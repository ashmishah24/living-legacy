## Proposed voice
Warm, calm, respectful, precise. Invite reflection without guilt or mortality pressure. Explain choices in everyday language. Avoid claiming permanence, perfect security, historical truth, or guaranteed recovery. The product should distinguish an unsaved local preview from a successfully preserved original.

## Proposed business narrative
Every family holds stories in different forms: a recording, a letter, a photograph, a remembered detail. Living Legacy brings those originals into a private place where their meaning can grow without losing the source. Owners can add context, review refinements, and choose which memories become a story. The aim is to make the record both personal and trustworthy: a story you can read, and the original moments you can return to.

This is new draft positioning based on the repository, not a recovered founder statement.

## Proposed microcopy
- Local preview: “Ready to review. This recording has not been saved to your archive.”
- Uploading: “Saving your original recording…”
- Preserved: “Original saved. You can return to it in your archive.”
- Transcript pending: “Your recording is saved. The transcript is still being prepared.”
- Transcript failed: “Your original is safe in the archive. We couldn’t prepare the transcript. Try again.” Use this only after durable source storage is verified.
- AI refinement: “AI-assisted draft. Compare it with the original before using it.”
- Narrative selection: “Only sources you approve can be added to this story.”
